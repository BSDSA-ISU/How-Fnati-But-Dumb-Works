## Little Help

- Press space to stand still.
- Only one camera can be shut off at a time. Shut off a camera by clicking the camera icon.
- Click on the light bulb to shut off the lights.
- You can stand still while the lights are off at the same time.

## Silent Help

- "Silent" doesn't mean silent sound cues.
- There is an indicator showing who is going to get buffed via hallucinations, sound cues, or both (depending on settings).
- Each hour, 3 random characters receive a silent buff for 30 seconds, for example:
  - 1 AM: Oswald buff, Pete buff, and Mortimer buff.
  - 2 AM: Classic The Face, The Face, and Mascot PNM.
  - And so on for 3 and 4 AM.
  - 5 AM (aka Eternal Hour): All characters get buffed one by one until all of them are buffed. Doesn't end until 6 AM (5:50 to 6:30 is where they get buffed one by one, and 6:30 to 7:00 is where all of them are buffed).
- Characters will return to normal if the silent effect ends.
- Characters cannot be buffed again until 5 AM if they were buffed before.
- The silent effect (not the buff) lasts 30 seconds.
- In order for a character to lose their buff, they need to finish their attack cycles. For example:
  - PNM starts his attack cycle on Cam-6; if the silent effect ends while PNM is on Cam-6, he stays buffed until you deal with him.
- Power Mechanic Mode: Silent mode introduces a power/energy mechanic where 100% == 10,000 in true value. After 6 seconds have passed, usage is increased by 1, and after every subsequent 3 seconds, another usage is added. The drain equation per tick is `usage * 3`. This usage is reset back to 0 by shutting off the camera with the green dot, which then moves to another random camera.

Here is how each character works:

## The Office Cycle Characters

### Mascot Photo Negative Mickey

- You need to flick your cursor horizontally until he leaves. The flick speed doesn't matter as long as it is not too slow. Disappears after 2 seconds of flicking.
- Silent Buff: He will not leave your office until the silent effect ends; however, flicking your mouse even a bit resets his kill timer, and the animation of the monitor going up and down pauses his kill timer. Can't kill you on cameras.
- Kills in 6 seconds now!
- Special: While flicking, his kill timer is paused and he can't kill you on cameras.
- Good News: Don't stand still for 6 seconds and you will never die at all.
- Bad News: Assuming he is buffed results in death.
- Note: On all silent buffs, MPNM gets deleted in the office after the silent effect ends, reappearing if Undying spawns so they don't get unfairly desynced.

### Undying

- Stand still on the right side of the office.
- Special: Kill timer is paused when standing still anywhere.
- Silent Buff: Has a 15-pixel margin of error, so you need to stand still on the very right of the office.
- Kills in 8 seconds.

### Acephalous

- Stand still on the left side of the office to get rid of him.
- Special: Kill timer is paused when standing still anywhere.
- Silent Buff: Same as Undying, but on the left side.
- Kills in 8 seconds.

### Sparky

- Shut off the lights, stand still anywhere, and then shut off any camera to get rid of him.
- Special: Kill timer is paused when standing still anywhere while the lights are off.
- Silent Buff: Barks and pans on the left or right side. Shut off the camera matching the side he barks on; shutting off the wrong camera results in instant death.
- Kills in 11.5 seconds (office and camera phases separately, so 23 seconds total).

## Distraction Characters

### Oswald

- Appears if you flip the monitor enough times; only spawns on flip-down. Stand still to get rid of him.
- Silent Buff: Will be forced into the office if 5 seconds pass on cameras, but he cannot spawn if you spawn him the normal way (by flipping the monitor up and down 2 times, then flipping up again without flipping down).
- Special: Kill timer is paused when standing still.
- Kills in 5 seconds (I think).

### Classic The Face

- Appears in the office after you flip the monitor down enough times. Open the monitor and drop it down to get rid of him, just like UCN Golden Freddy.
- Silent Buff: Requires you to be on cameras for 0.3 seconds.
- Kills in 2 seconds.
- Special: Stalls everybody's kill timer except Oswald if he is attacking.
- The Good News: There is a bug with Classic The Face where it doesn't spawn during the silent effect.

### Pete

- Appears as a dot on cameras. Watch it for 1.5 seconds until it disappears.
- Special: Kicks out Willie if it spawns on the same camera as Willie.
- Silent Buff: Moves every two seconds on cameras; watching him pauses his movement and he leaves normally.
- Kills in 15 seconds (I think).
- Bad News: Easily forgettable if time hits 5 AM.

### Disembodied

- Will scream on your left or right side. Shut off the camera corresponding to the side he is attacking on.
- Silent Buff: Takes 0.25 seconds before he leaves; dealing with him too fast doesn't get rid of him.
- Special: Cannot appear if any characters are attacking in the office, preventing unfair deaths.
- Cannot kill you, but disables standing still and shutting off lights.

### Willie

- Appears on a random camera. Do not stare for too long or he will progress.
- Silent Buff: He is on every single camera at the same time, so cycle cameras very fast to prevent it. Goes away if the silent effect ends.
- Kills if you watch him for too long in his Stage 3.

## And Finally, The Big Six (Hardest Characters)

### Photo Negative Mickey

- Shut off Cam-6 if he is on the left side of the office, or Cam-2 if he is on the right side of the office (takes two seconds for both).
- Silent Buff: Requires you to shut off the camera and stand still, but you only need to stand still for 0.5 seconds. Resets his kill timer to 4 seconds if you shut off his camera and he has a lower than 4-second kill time.
- Special: Kill timer is paused when his camera is shut off.
- Kills in 6 seconds (I think).

### The Face

- Shut off the lights if he appears in your office.
- Silent Buff: Shut off the lights before he enters the office. If failed, he kills you after 5 seconds no matter what. Still has a sound cue warning before he enters, just like normal and aggressive. Pure evil! You can no longer use his stalling mechanic anymore.
- Kills in 5 seconds.
- Special: Stalls everybody's kill timer if he is in the office (except Oswald, Mortimer, and Impurity), and the kill timer is paused when the lights are shut off.

### Classic Photo Negative Mickey

- Appears in Cam-6, Cam-2, Cam-1, and Cam-3. Shut off the camera where he was last seen when he attacks.
- Silent Buff: He can now appear in every single camera, but still has 28 seconds before he attacks.
- Kills in 6 seconds (I think).

### Impurity

- Moves through Cam-3 -> Cam-1 -> Cam-2 -> Cam-6 -> to your office. If she reaches your office, you are guaranteed to die. Shut off her camera where she is currently located to instantly reset her move timer to 11 seconds.
- Silent Buff: Turns her camera back on and shuts off a random camera.
- Special: Kills you if she is in the office, and stalls if Sparky attacks during the camera phase.

### Hourglass

- Oh boy: if he is on Cam-9, shut off the light; if he is on Cam-3 or Cam-4, shut off any camera; if he is on Cam-5 or Cam-8, stand still.
- Kills in 6 seconds.
- Special: Kill timer is paused while performing the correct action.
- Silent Buff: Moves to another camera after 12 seconds, then attacks after 12 seconds.
- Note: You have enough time to do all actions even if you don't remember where he is. Just make sure to stand still while the lights are off to save time.
- The Good News: The "do everything" cheese still works since this is not aggressive mode.

### Mortimer

- Appears in your office and starts playing his song "Suicidemouse.avi". Search the cameras and shut off the camera where the song is the loudest, and he will disappear.
- Silent Buff: After 1.5 seconds of his camera being closed, his kill timer will gradually increase by +14 each second and goes away if it reaches 24 seconds.
- Special: Kill timer is paused when his camera is shut off; cannot be stalled by others.
- Kills in 24 seconds.
- The Good News: You only need to deal with him once since he leaves quickly (this isn't aggressive).

## Useful Notes

- CPNM is 28 seconds slow, so you have time to cycle all cameras to find him (aggressive CPNM is still harder).
- Hourglass "try everything" strat still works, so aggressive Hourglass is harder.
- MPNM basically can't kill you, but assuming he is buffed results in death.
- Undying is Acephalous: just stand still on the very left/right always.
